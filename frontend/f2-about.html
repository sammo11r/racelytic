Racelytic’s Formula 2 archive connects seasons, race weekends, sessions, drivers, constructors and circuits in one explorable record.

01

More than final standings

Formula 2 weekends combine practice, qualifying, sprint races and feature races. Racelytic preserves that structure so a championship result can always be traced back to the sessions that shaped it.

The archive is built for exploring progression: who raced for each team, where drivers scored their points, how constructors developed, and which venues defined each season.

03

Built from recorded results

The Formula 2 archive is assembled from the project dataset and Motorsport Stats classifications. Import tools keep session results, championship points and award markers connected to the local database.

Motorsport records can contain corrections, renamed teams and changing event formats. Racelytic aims to preserve those distinctions transparently and may be updated when better source data becomes available.

PROJECT PRINCIPLES
  • CompleteRepresent the full classified field rather than only podium finishers.
  • ConnectedLink standings back to weekends, sessions and competitors.
  • TransparentKeep cancellations, missing data and source limitations visible.
04

Independent by design
