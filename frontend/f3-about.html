ABOUT RACELYTIC F3

Formula 3 history,
session by session.

Racelytic’s Formula 3 archive connects seasons, race weekends, classifications, drivers, teams and circuits in one structured dataset.

01

Built beyond the podium

The archive preserves the complete classified field for practice, qualifying, sprint and feature sessions rather than recording only the leading finishers.

Cancelled sessions remain visible, substitutions stay connected to their event entries, and championship points can be traced back to individual weekends.

03

Collected from recorded results

The dataset is assembled from Motorsport Stats season, event, entry, classification and standings data, with official Formula 3 sources used to complete missing nationality information.

Motorsport records can contain later corrections and source limitations. Racelytic keeps the importer repeatable so the archive can be reviewed and updated.

PROJECT PRINCIPLES
  • CompleteRepresent every available classified competitor.
  • ConnectedLink standings, entries, sessions and results.
  • TransparentKeep cancellations and missing values visible.
