ABOUT RACELYTIC

Motorsport history,
connected.

Racelytic is an independent project that turns scattered racing records into a structured, explorable history.

01

Why it exists

Motorsport history is usually encountered one result at a time. Standings live apart from the races that shaped them, careers cross teams and championships, and changing formats make different eras difficult to compare.

Racelytic brings those records together so the path from a championship table to an individual session remains visible. The aim is not simply to collect results, but to preserve the relationships and context that make them meaningful.

02

From records to an archive

COLLECT

Start with recorded results

Public datasets and published classifications provide the foundation. Sources vary by championship and are credited where their data is used.

CONNECT

Preserve the relationships

Seasons, events, sessions, competitors, teams and venues are linked so a summary can always lead back to its underlying records.

INTERPRET

Make assumptions visible

Names and formats are normalized for consistency, while cancellations, substitutions, missing values and regulation changes remain part of the historical context.

03

Project principles

The same standards guide the database, analysis and simulation layers.

01

Connected

Keep every statistic close to the results and relationships behind it.

02

Transparent

Show rules, filters, limitations and analytical assumptions clearly.

03

Contextual

Respect the formats and circumstances that distinguish championships and eras.

04

Exploratory

Create room for questions and comparisons instead of presenting only conclusions.

04

A growing historical record

Racelytic grows championship by championship, with depth taking priority over simply adding more series. New records and tools are integrated when they can remain connected to a dependable underlying structure.

Historical sources can be incomplete or corrected later. The archive is therefore an evolving interpretation of the best available information, not an official record or final authority.

INDEPENDENT PROJECT
