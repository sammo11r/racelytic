TEAM HANDBOOK

How to build a winning operation

Every decision feeds the next. Race for resources, invest where the team is weakest and take on tougher opposition when the operation is ready.

Core loop
  1. Race
  2. Earn
  3. Develop
  4. Attract sponsors
  5. Hire staff
  6. Unlock circuits
01

Choose the next race

Compare entry costs, lap count and circuit demands. New venues require both reputation and a sufficiently strong driver.

02

Race from the back

Your car starts every event in P20. Its development, condition, driver and engineer determine how far it can climb.

03

Develop and maintain

Spend earnings on connected upgrade paths. Service the car before its condition becomes too low to enter.

04

Find the right sponsor

Sponsors multiply race winnings when you meet their finishing target. Better results and reputation attract stronger contracts.

05

Build the team

Drivers improve race performance. Reputation and research unlock engineers who raise the development ceiling and add a race bonus.

06

Climb the ladder

Reputation opens markets and circuits. Balance immediate race costs against upgrades that make future events more profitable.

RACE-DAY CHECK

Confirm the entry cost, car condition, sponsor target and circuit strengths before pressing start. The race playback cannot be skipped.

Idle Racing Manager takes place in an original fictional motorsport world. Every team, driver, sponsor and circuit appearing in the game is fictional.
