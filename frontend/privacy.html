1. Who is responsible

Sam Nijsten, resident in the Netherlands, operates Racelytic and is the controller of personal data processed through racelytic.com. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to racelytic@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

When you create an account, we process your username, display name, password hash, account timestamps, the versions of the Terms and Privacy Notice you accepted, and the acceptance time. We never store your plain-text password.

If you use account features, we store your saved points systems, records and custom championships. Items you mark public are shown with your display name. Private items remain visible only to your account.

For authentication and security, we process a session token in a necessary cookie, its hash and expiry time, and temporarily use your IP address in memory to limit abusive login and analytics requests. The application does not write IP addresses to its analytics database.

If you choose analytics, we store a randomly generated visitor identifier, page path without query parameters, referring website host, visit time and active duration. We do not use advertising trackers or sell personal data.

3. Why we process data

  • Providing your account and saved features: necessary to perform our agreement with you under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Authentication, abuse prevention and service security: necessary to provide the service and for our legitimate interest in protecting Racelytic and its users under Article 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Optional audience analytics: based on your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Refusing analytics does not restrict the website.
  • Responding to privacy requests: necessary to meet legal obligations under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

4. Hosting, recipients and transfers

Racelytic is hosted on a STRATO virtual private server in Germany, with its application database hosted there. STRATO processes hosting and infrastructure data on our behalf. Messages sent to our contact address are processed through Google as our email provider.

We do not intend to transfer application or analytics data outside the European Economic Area. Email processing by Google may involve processing outside the EEA under the safeguards made available by that provider. We disclose data only when required by law, needed to protect the service or handled by a service provider acting for us.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account data and saved creations remain until you delete them or request account deletion.
  • Login sessions expire after 30 days and can end earlier when you sign out.
  • Optional analytics visits are automatically deleted after 13 months.
  • Short-lived rate-limit records are held in server memory for up to 15 minutes for account attempts and 60 seconds for analytics requests.
  • Hosting security and access logs are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for operation, troubleshooting and security, according to the server configuration.
  • Privacy correspondence is retained as long as needed to resolve and document the request.

6. Your rights

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw analytics consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Send requests to racelytic@gmail.com. We may need to verify that the request concerns your account. You can also complain to the Dutch supervisory authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or another competent EEA supervisory authority.

7. Cookies and local storage

The racelytics_session cookie is strictly necessary to keep you signed in. It is HTTP-only, uses SameSite=Lax and is marked Secure in production. Racelytic also stores your Formula 1 or Formula 2 interface preference locally.

Optional analytics starts only after you select “Allow analytics”. That choice and, if allowed, a random visitor ID are stored in your browser. The choice expires after 13 months and the identifier is removed when you next visit. You can change the choice at any time using “Privacy settings” in the footer. Selecting “Decline” removes the analytics visitor ID immediately. We also honour an enabled Do Not Track browser signal.

8. Children

Racelytic accounts are intended for people aged 16 or older. Do not create an account if you are under 16.

9. Changes

We may update this notice when the service or law changes. We will update the effective date and, where required, provide additional notice or request a new acknowledgement.
