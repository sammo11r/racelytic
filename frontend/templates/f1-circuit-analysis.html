Data & methodology

Grands Prix only; sprint races are excluded. DNS, DNQ, DNPQ and withdrawn entries are excluded from starts. Retirements include recorded DNF results and retirement reasons, including late retirements that were classified; disqualifications are excluded from retirements. Pole conversion uses races with a recorded pole starter. Missing values remain unavailable.

Time margins use recorded milliseconds when available, otherwise the recorded time gap. Lapped finishes are listed separately. Historical layout changes, shortened races and different regulations affect comparisons; use year filters to narrow the context. Source: F1DB.
