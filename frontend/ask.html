What can I ask?

Racelytic answers these questions from recorded results.

Records and results

  • Career record rankingsRank drivers or constructors by wins, podiums, poles, points and more.
  • Individual record totalsCalculate one driver or constructor’s total for a selected record.
  • Race resultsLook up a winner, podium, classification or one driver’s result.
  • Season standingsShow final standings or the championship order after a round.
  • Longest streaksRank consecutive wins, podiums, points finishes or classified finishes.

Profiles and explanations

  • Driver profilesSummarize a driver’s career, teams, results and championships.
  • Constructor profilesExplore a team’s seasons, drivers, results and titles.
  • Circuit profilesSee a venue’s location, archive span and leading winners.
  • Season summariesReview champions, race counts, winners and leading standings.

Comparisons

  • Driver head-to-headsCompare two drivers across careers, shared starts or teammate races.
  • Constructor head-to-headsCompare two constructors or teams using official archive totals.

Historical scoring

  • Recalculated championship totalsRank title totals under a historical Formula 1 rulebook.
  • Recalculated career pointsRank cumulative points after rescoring every completed season with one rulebook.
  • Recalculated season championsApply another points system to one completed season.
  • Recalculated driver or constructor titlesCount one competitor’s titles under another rulebook.
  • Changed championshipsFind seasons whose champion changes under another system.
  • Points-system comparisonsCompare two complete historical Formula 1 rulebooks.
