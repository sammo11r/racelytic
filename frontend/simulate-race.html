    HOW TO USE THE REPLAY

    Follow the race, not just the result.

    The replay turns recorded race data into a moving overview of the whole field, so strategy shifts, recoveries and changing gaps are easier to understand.

    01

    Choose an event

    Pick a season and Grand Prix. The circuit, starting order and complete race timeline load automatically.

    02

    Shape the field

    Open the Drivers menu to isolate a battle, compare teammates or keep all cars visible for the full picture.

    03

    Control the pace

    Pause at a key moment, jump ten seconds ahead or accelerate long stretches without losing the running order.

    BEHIND THE REPLAY

    A clearer view of how the race developed.

    Driver markers are reconstructed from recorded position coordinates and synchronized with lap timing, status and classification data. The timing tower confirms short-lived position changes before displaying them, keeping close battles readable without hiding genuine overtakes.
