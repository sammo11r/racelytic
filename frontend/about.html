ABOUT RACELYTICS

Formula 1 history,
made explorable.

Racelytics is an independent Formula 1 database and analysis platform built to make decades of results easier to explore, compare and understand.

01

Beyond the final result

A classification tells you who finished where. Racelytics helps reveal the larger story: how a championship developed, how teammates compared, where drivers performed best and how different rules could have changed an outcome.

The project brings historical records, interactive analysis and championship simulation into one consistent interface. It is designed for curious fans who want to move naturally from a headline statistic to the races behind it.

03

Built on open data

Racelytics uses the community-maintained F1DB dataset. Its breadth makes it possible to connect more than seventy years of championships, events, people, teams and machinery.

Historical motorsport data is rarely perfectly uniform. Regulations, formats and record-keeping have changed considerably over time, so calculations are presented as analytical interpretations of the available data—not as official rulings.

PROJECT PRINCIPLES
  • ConnectedMove easily between a statistic and its source results.
  • TransparentMake rules, filters and assumptions visible.
  • ExploratoryEncourage questions instead of presenting only conclusions.
