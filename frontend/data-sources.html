Our approach

Racelytic turns published motorsport records into a connected archive for historical research, analysis and championship calculations. Source names and links are provided for transparency. A citation does not by itself grant a licence, transfer ownership, or imply that a source endorses Racelytic.

Results and derived calculations can contain transcription errors, source errors or later corrections. Racelytic is not an official record; important facts should be checked against the relevant championship documents.

Formula 1

Formula 1 results, championship records and circuit SVGs are adapted from F1DB, created by Marcel Overdijk, with circuit artwork by Jules Roy and contributions from the F1DB community.

F1DB is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. Racelytic normalises identifiers and names, restructures the source tables, corrects selected records and derives additional statistics. Changes were made; Racelytic’s modifications are not endorsed by the F1DB contributors.

Formula 2

The Formula 2 archive is compiled from published classifications, including Motorsport Stats, selected official FIA event documents, and project-maintained corrections. Racelytic converts these records into a consistent event, driver and team structure and calculates its own summaries.

These references identify provenance only. Third-party website content, document layouts, names, marks and other protected material remain subject to the rights and terms of their owners.

Formula 3

The Formula 3 archive is compiled from published classifications, including Motorsport Stats, official championship material and project-maintained corrections. Racelytic normalises the records and derives standings and analysis from the resulting data.

These references identify provenance only. Third-party website content, document layouts, names, marks and other protected material remain subject to the rights and terms of their owners.

F1 Academy

The F1 Academy archive is compiled from calendars and classifications published on the official F1 Academy website, supplemented by project-maintained corrections. Racelytic normalises driver, team, circuit and event records and derives standings and analysis.

The official website and its materials are governed by the publisher’s own terms. Linking and attribution do not imply endorsement or grant Racelytic rights in its protected content.

Formula E

The Formula E archive is compiled from calendars, entry lists and classifications published through the official FIA Formula E results archive. Racelytic normalises two-year season labels, drivers, teams, manufacturers, circuits, sessions and E-Prix classifications, then derives connected statistics and analysis.

Qualifying is preserved as the official displayed classification. Historical Attack Mode activation data is not available in the classification archive, and unavailable manufacturer standings are not inferred from team results.

World Endurance Championship

The WEC archive is compiled from calendars and championship tables published on the official FIA WEC website, plus the official Al Kamel timing classifications. Racelytic models each classified car once, then links its event-specific driver crew, team, manufacturer, car model and class.

The published archive covers all 220 race-weekend sessions across the 2022–2025 seasons. It preserves 2022 LMGTE Pro and LMP2 Pro/Am, the full-season LMP2 and LMGTE Am fields through 2023, the Le Mans-only LMP2 entries in later seasons, and the 2023 Garage 56 Innovative Car. Drivers’, teams’ and manufacturers’ standings progression uses the official published event-points cells and is not reconstructed from finishing positions.

Changes and corrections

Racelytic may standardise spelling, identifiers, country codes, team names, race formats and classification statuses so records can be compared across seasons. Some missing grid positions are derived from qualifying results and the documented race format; pages identify derived values where they are displayed.

If a source changes or a discrepancy is found, Racelytic may update the archive. Please include a reliable supporting source when suggesting a correction.
